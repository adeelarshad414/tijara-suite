# Tijara Suite Demo Video Script

## Scene: Intro

**Duration**: 15 seconds

**Visual**: Odoo login, then the Tijara app shell and POS surfaces.

**Voiceover**: "Tijara Suite is an open-source-first retail and restaurant platform for Pakistan, combining POS, inventory, SaaS controls, analytics, hardware readiness, and Urdu-friendly operations on Odoo Community."

**Transition**: Cut to POS checkout.

## Scene: Cashier POS

**Duration**: 60 seconds

**Visual**: POS checkout, B2B/B2C pricing, bill discount, receipt print, refund barcode scan.

**Voiceover**: "As a cashier, you can sell to walk-in or wholesale customers, switch B2B and B2C pricing, apply a bill discount by amount or percentage, print a receipt through the local bridge, and scan invoice barcodes for refund or exchange."

**Transition**: Cut to kiosk.

## Scene: Restaurant Kiosk And Queue

**Duration**: 60 seconds

**Visual**: Kiosk menu, dine-in/takeaway/pickup choice, queue display, kitchen ticket flow.

**Voiceover**: "As a restaurant operator, you can run self-ordering kiosk checkout for dine-in, takeaway, and pickup, publish menu and deals screens, issue queue tickets, and move orders through preparation and ready states."

**Transition**: Cut to inventory.

## Scene: Inventory Intelligence

**Duration**: 45 seconds

**Visual**: Low-stock alerts, expiry alerts, storage positions, bulk import/export.

**Voiceover**: "As an inventory manager, you can track stock by warehouse, rack, shelf, and bin, review low-stock and expiry alerts, and use CSV import and export for bulk product and stock operations."

**Transition**: Cut to accounting.

## Scene: Accounting And Compliance

**Duration**: 45 seconds

**Visual**: PSP settlements, refunds and chargebacks, draft accounting actions, FBR queue.

**Voiceover**: "As an accountant, you can review payment webhooks, reconcile provider settlements, manage refunds and chargebacks, create guarded draft moves, and track FBR submission readiness."

**Transition**: Cut to analytics.

## Scene: Dashboards And SaaS Control

**Duration**: 50 seconds

**Visual**: Analytics dashboards, KPI history, SaaS plans, feature flags, tenant provisioning.

**Voiceover**: "As a tenant admin or platform operator, you can monitor sales, inventory, queue, and promotion KPIs, enable SaaS features such as B2B, queue, customer display, and promotion display, and prepare tenant provisioning evidence."

**Transition**: Cut to release evidence.

## Scene: Production Readiness

**Duration**: 35 seconds

**Visual**: Protected release gates, evidence scorecard, deployment docs, monitoring stack.

**Voiceover**: "For enterprise deployment, Tijara includes release gates, protected-runner evidence, hardware certification records, monitoring, backup drills, security checks, and deployment runbooks."

**Transition**: Fade to closing.

## Scene: Closing

**Duration**: 20 seconds

**Visual**: Public display screens and POS checkout summary.

**Voiceover**: "Tijara Suite gives Pakistani businesses a modular open-source foundation for stores, pharmacies, restaurants, bakeries, garment shops, electronics retailers, groceries, superstores, and wholesale operations."

**Transition**: Fade out.
